# Convex Optimization: Convex sets

Proofs and counterexamples: affine sets as solution sets of linear equations, convexity of slabs, wedges and distance-defined sets, convexity of sets of probability distributions, uniqueness of the projection onto a compact convex set, and the l-infinity ball as a polyhedron.
