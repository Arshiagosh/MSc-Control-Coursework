\summaryheader{Multivariable Control, Homework 8}{Reading Assignments: Multivariable Feedback Design}{Autumn 1403 (2024)}

This homework was a set of reading questions on course notes about multivariable feedback design. Each section ended with a summary of at most two pages. My summaries cover:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Design objectives.} Good command following, disturbance rejection and tolerance to modelling error, written as conditions on the singular values of the sensitivity and complementary sensitivity functions. Also steady-state error and closed-loop stability.
  \item \textbf{Normality.} How normal and non-normal plants affect performance, and modelling the plant through matrix decompositions.
  \item \textbf{Model-based controllers.} Stability analysis in the time and frequency domains.
\end{enumerate}
Each part gave practical conclusions on choosing loop shapes for MIMO controllers.
